Resume Screener

An easy-to-use tool to match resumes with job descriptions. Recruiters post jobs and rank applicants by match score. Candidates upload resumes and see their results.

Register as Recruiter Register as Candidate
1. Candidates Apply

Candidates choose an open job and submit their PDF or Word (.docx) resume from their portal.

2. Calculate Match Score

Compares resume skills with job requirements using TF-IDF and shows a percentage score from 0% to 100%.

3. Background Worker

Uses a background task queue so resumes are processed smoothly without slowing down the website.

4. Schedule Interviews

Recruiters shortlist candidates and set interview dates and meeting links in one click.
